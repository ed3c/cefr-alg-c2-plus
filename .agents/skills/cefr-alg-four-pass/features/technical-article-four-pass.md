# Technical article four-pass learning note

## Sub-features

- source claim freeze and stable provenance IDs;
- low-friction context pass;
- precise C2 technical English pass;
- transcript-hidden listening reconstruction;
- active retell/back-translation and mutation;
- source-bound oracle comparison;
- small active technical vocabulary set.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Open the Technical Article Lab from CEFR ALG C2+ and choose a compiled article. Start at
Pass 1 and move forward when ready. No pass is locked by a score.

## Driving it with the website

Passes 1-3 can be read and played. Pass 3 starts with its transcript hidden. Pass 4 shows
prompts before the oracle. The learner explicitly reveals the oracle after attempting an
answer and may use the active-vocabulary list for another retell.

The site does not upload the learner's answer or grade it.

## Gotchas

A shorter pass may not drop a condition or uncertainty. C2+ is not a certification result.
Four mechanical repetitions are not the four-pass method. A generated audio track is a
reading aid, not evidence of comprehension. The oracle compares meaning, not exact wording.
